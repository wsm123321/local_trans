# Attempt 1 Rejected

This directory is a completed code-validation attempt, but it is excluded from all scientific conclusions.

Independent review found three design confounds:

1. actionable feedback rounds evaluated a source-selected counterproposal, so the feedback sampling distribution depended on the mechanism under test;
2. Local Spearman consumed all point observations while disagreement correction consumed at most one round-level event, so evidence units were not decision-matched;
3. ineligible prediction scores were mapped to zero, conflating abstention with evidence of no future benefit in AUPRC.

The revised v1-R protocol uses fixed source-blind target-acquisition diagnostic pairs, decision-matched pair-level evidence for all three estimators, primary AUPRC on their common eligible support, and neutral-imputed AUPRC only as sensitivity analysis. Attempt 1 artifacts are retained for audit and are never merged with v1-R outputs.
